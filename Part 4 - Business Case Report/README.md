# Part 4: Business Case Report and Recommendations

## Overview

This section contains our completed business case report, bringing together the data preparation, Tableau visualizations, and predictive analysis conducted throughout the project.

The report examines how historical food inspection records can help Montgomery County identify recurring safety concerns and potentially improve the allocation of inspection resources.

## Main Findings

Our analysis identified several important patterns:

- Adverse inspection outcomes varied considerably across different establishment categories.
- Cold holding temperatures and pest control were two of the most frequent food safety concerns.
- Refrigeration-related violations increased during warmer months.
- Previous inspection outcomes provided useful information for predicting future inspection risks.
- Overall predictive accuracy did not fully reflect the model's ability to detect adverse outcomes.

## Recommendations

Based on our findings, we developed three recommendations.

**1. Prioritize Repeat Violators**

Establishments with previous adverse inspection outcomes should receive additional attention and timely follow-up inspections.

**2. Improve Risk-Based Inspection Planning**

Health departments could evaluate predictive models and alternative classification thresholds to better identify establishments that may require earlier inspection.

**3. Strengthen Preventive Maintenance**

Encouraging refrigeration inspections and equipment maintenance before summer could help reduce temperature-related food safety problems.

## Final Perspective

Our project demonstrates that business analytics can offer meaningful benefits beyond traditional business settings. By combining historical inspection data, visual analysis, and predictive modeling, public health departments may be able to make more informed decisions about where to focus their limited resources.

While predictive models cannot replace professional judgment, they can provide additional information to support safer and more effective inspection practices.
