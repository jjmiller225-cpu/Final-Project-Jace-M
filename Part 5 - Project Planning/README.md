# Part 5: Project Planning and Research Development

## Overview

This section provides the background and planning documents that guided our Montgomery County food inspection analysis. Before creating visualizations and developing our predictive model, our team established research objectives, examined the available inspection data, and developed a structured approach to answering our main questions.

These documents demonstrate how we organized the project from its initial concept into a complete business analytics study.

## Project Overview Document

**File:** `Overview of Project.docx`

This document introduces the Montgomery County food inspection dataset and outlines our original research direction.

It includes:

- Background information about the inspection records
- Description of the dataset and available variables
- Initial research questions
- Limitations and considerations affecting the analysis

The overview helped establish what we wanted to investigate and ensured that our research questions could be answered using the available data.

## Project Task Plan

**File:** `Project Task Plan.docx`

Our task plan outlines the methods and analytical approaches selected to address the project's three main research questions.

These include:

1. **Inspection Outcomes:** Comparing adverse inspection rates across establishment categories and inspection types.
2. **Food Safety Violations:** Identifying frequently violated requirements and examining patterns across facilities and seasons.
3. **Predictive Analysis:** Using logistic regression to investigate whether previous inspection results and establishment characteristics could help predict future adverse outcomes.

The plan also identifies the visualizations and statistical methods intended for each question.

## Purpose of This Section

Including these documents provides a record of the research process and demonstrates how our team connected the initial project objectives to the final findings and recommendations.

Together, they show the planning and decision-making that supported our analysis.
