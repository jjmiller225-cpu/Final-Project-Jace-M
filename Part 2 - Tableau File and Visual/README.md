# Part 2: Tableau Visualizations and Inspection Patterns

## Overview

This section presents our Tableau analysis of food safety inspection outcomes across Montgomery County. We used visualizations to make the inspection data easier to interpret and to identify patterns that might otherwise be overlooked when examining individual records.

## Visualization Objectives

Our analysis focused on three main questions:

1. Which types of food establishments experience the highest rates of adverse inspection outcomes?
2. Which food safety requirements are most frequently violated?
3. How do inspection outcomes and compliance issues change across establishment categories and seasons?

## Key Findings

Our visualizations showed that public middle schools (40.5%), restaurants (36.2%), and carry-out establishments (33.2%) experienced relatively high adverse inspection rates.

Cold holding temperature violations were among the most common compliance issues, appearing in approximately 19.9% of inspections. Rodent and insect violations followed at 15.5%.

We also observed an increase in cold holding problems during the summer, suggesting that seasonal conditions may contribute to refrigeration-related challenges.
